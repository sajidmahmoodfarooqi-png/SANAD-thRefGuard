## Posting tips
- **Put the link in the first comment**, not the post body — LinkedIn tends to show link-in-body posts to fewer people. Say "link in comments," then add the URL as the first comment right after posting.
- **Best time:** a weekday morning (Tue–Thu) in your audience's timezone.
- **First line is everything** — LinkedIn only shows ~2 lines before "…see more," so the hook stays at the very top (it does).
- **Tag & reach:** tag a few relevant colleagues, your university/department page, and any research-methods or PhD groups you're in. Consider co-posting in relevant LinkedIn groups.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first few hours — engagement early widens reach.
- **Pin it** to your profile featured section afterward so visitors see it.
- Repost/refresh in ~2 weeks with an update ("X people have tried it; here's what testers found") — momentum compounds.
