SANAD the RefGuard

This page hosts the Microsoft Word task-pane add-in only — the pane's interface files. It is not the application itself.

The task pane loads at /taskpane.html and talks solely to your local SANAD engine on 127.0.0.1. Your document never leaves your machine.

SANAD is free and open-source (AGPLv3). Project & downloads: github.com/sajidmahmoodfarooqi-png/SANAD-thRefGuard
